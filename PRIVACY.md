# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** June 11, 2026

Welcome to **P2PFather**. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we process, store, and share, and how we protect your security when using our P2P trading platform and Telegram Mini App.

---

## 1. Our Privacy Commitment
P2PFather is designed as a **privacy-first** peer-to-peer trading service. We believe in minimal data collection. We do not track you, sell your data, or collect unnecessary information that could put our team or our users at legal, privacy, or security risk.

---

## 2. Information We Do NOT Collect
To ensure maximum safety and anonymity:
- **No IP Addresses:** We do not collect, log, or store your IP address.
- **No User Agents:** We do not log or store browser fingerprint data, device types, or User Agent strings.
- **No Traditional KYC:** We do not require or collect your real name, email address, physical address, or government-issued ID documents.

---

## 3. Information We Process & Store
We only process and store data that is strictly necessary to run the P2P marketplace, escrow smart contracts, and dispute resolution:

- **Telegram Identity:** We associate your account with your Telegram ID, Telegram Username, and First Name. This is required to identify you in the Mini App, manage active orders, and send system updates via our Telegram Bot.
- **Wallet Addresses:** We store your wallet address (either the Derived Custodial Bot Wallet or your linked External Wallet) to check balances, coordinate escrow locks, and release funds.
- **UPI ID and Phone Number:** We collect your UPI ID and/or Phone Number to enable order creation and trade execution. These details are stored in our secure database and are shared **only** with your counterparty during an active trade to facilitate the P2P fiat transfer.
- **Dispute and Chat Evidence:** If a trade enters a dispute, the messages, uploaded screenshots (payment proofs), and UTRs (Unique Transaction References) are stored and accessed only by platform admins to resolve the dispute fairly.

---

## 4. Blockchain & Public Data
When you lock or release funds in escrow, these actions interact with public blockchains (such as Base or BNB Smart Chain). 
- **Public Record:** Wallet addresses, deposit amounts, transaction hashes, and on-chain trade statuses are permanently recorded on the public ledger.
- **Immutability:** Once written, blockchain data is public and cannot be modified, deleted, or censored by us or any third party.

---

## 5. Third-Party Services
Our app integrates with third-party networks which operate under their own privacy policies:
- **Telegram Messenger:** The interface is hosted as a Telegram Mini App. Telegram collects data in accordance with its privacy terms.
- **RPC Providers & Nodes:** Interacting with the blockchain routes requests through public or private node providers (such as Alchemy or public RPCs).
- **LI.FI Bridge:** If you use the in-app bridging service, transaction details are processed by the LI.FI protocol.

---

## 6. Data Security
- **Secure Encrypted Connections:** All traffic between your client and our API is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS.
- **Smart Contract Safety:** Escrowed funds are secured using open-source, audited smart contracts rather than centralized databases.
- **HD Key Management:** Custodial wallets are generated programmatically using standard BIP-44 key derivation.

---

## 7. Data Retention & Control
You have direct control over your profile data:
- You can add, edit, or remove your UPI ID and payment details at any time via the Profile screen in the Mini App.
- Since we do not store traditional personal files, there is no tracker data to clear.

---

## 8. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our service or regulatory practices. Any updates will be announced directly through the Telegram channel or in the app's guide.

---

## 9. Contact Us
For questions regarding your privacy, security, or data handling, please contact the administrators through the **P2PFather Telegram Support Bot**.
